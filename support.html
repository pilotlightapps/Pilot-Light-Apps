Help with your app

Support

Need help with a Pilot Light app, found a problem, or have an idea? Get in touch.

Email support

You can also email support@pilotlightapps.com from your usual email service.

Tell us what happened

A few details make it easier to help:

  • The app name and version, and where you downloaded it.
  • Your Windows version.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message, or a screenshot with personal details hidden.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, or private photos or files. We do not need your whole photo collection to investigate a problem. See our privacy policy for how the apps and website handle information.

Help by app

Wallpaper Pilot shuffles local wallpapers. It does not support multi-monitor setups. Include your monitor setup and the folder-selection or shuffle behavior involved when reporting a problem.

Display / Audio Switcher switches saved display and playback-device profiles. Include the display and audio devices involved, and whether Windows can select them normally.

Picture Pilot Sorter is a public test release. Include the image formats involved and whether the issue happened during scanning, tagging, or building output folders. Keep your originals and test with a small sample first.

Downloads and updates

Use each app's page above for its current download, release notes, and known limitations. Mention where you installed the app from so we can help with the correct edition.
